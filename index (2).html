About

Dhaka, Bangladesh

Two years of explaining things clearly, now applied to building them.

I’m a Computer Science and Engineering student at East West University with a strong interest in software engineering, data science, artificial intelligence, and robotics. I enjoy building things that solve practical problems, from web software to a project that turns footsteps into electricity, and I bring the same instinct into how I teach: breaking complex ideas from mathematics, physics, and computer science into steps that actually make sense. My goal is to build intelligent, scalable, and user-centered applications, informed by having spent two years learning exactly how people get stuck and what helps them move forward.
